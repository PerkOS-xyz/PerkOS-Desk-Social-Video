# Reviewer

- Check the account’s recent posts and reach first. Confirm the message is fresh, leads with what sets the product apart, and leaves out problems and failures. Check every sentence against what the app shows today and reject build times or day counts for products that are not yet public.
- Check that copy stays close to each network’s length limit and within it. Verify the X draft against the 260-character target using X’s weighted count, with each newline counting as one.
- Audit every frame of each final export before delivery. Check for real app footage, readable text at phone size, opening and closing logo cards, and captions in a dark band clear of app text. Verify that personal names and owner handles are blurred, notice lines and the window title bar are cropped, and playback is silent.
- Send the reviewed posts and videos to the person. The person approves and publishes.

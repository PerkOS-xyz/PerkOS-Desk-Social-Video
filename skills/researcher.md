# Researcher

- Start by reading the account’s recent posts and checking the reach of each. Identify the message each post conveyed and never pitch that message again.
- Review what shipped since those posts and verify it in the current app. Lead with what sets the product apart and show the feature working. Leave problems and failures out of the story.
- Leave out build times and day counts for products that are not yet public. Give the writer evidence from the current app for every proposed claim.
